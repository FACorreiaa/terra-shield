# Visual inspiration catalog

These are **links to the outside reference images and products discussed in the conversation**. They are not bundled as downloaded screenshots because the third-party images were displayed as remote linked content rather than locally generated files; permissions for redistribution were not established. Open the links to view them. The 4 backgrounds included in `03_Backgrounds` are **new original files made specifically for this bundle**.

## Interfaces

- **Flighty** - premium, information-dense Apple UI; flight cards and dark interface. https://www.flightyapp.com/
- **Apple Weather** - native iOS/macOS weather experiences and precipitation visualization. https://www.apple.com/weather/
- **Watch Duty** - useful incident mapping, incident boundaries and urgent details. https://refs.gallery/apps/watch-duty
- **Earthquake Prediction Data / Dribbble** - monitoring dashboard layout reference. https://dribbble.com/shots/21471855-Earthquake-Prediction-Data

## Maps / background references

- **Windy** - weather layers and timeline visual language. https://www.windy.com/
- **Ventusky** - global globe and atmospheric motion. https://www.ventusky.com/
- **Zoom Earth** - cloud / storm and satellite visualization. https://zoom.earth/
- **NASA Black Marble / Earth at Night** - nocturnal planet imagery. https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/
- **CARTO Dark Matter** - subdued dark cartographic basemap. https://www.carto.com/basemaps/
- **MapLibre** - vector maps for a custom dark style. https://maplibre.org/

## Design guidance

Use inspiration for layout, density, atmospheric treatment and map layer hierarchy, **not direct pixel-for-pixel copies, copied trademarks or borrowed assets**.

The product should prioritize reliable source data, genuine hazard information, contrasting warnings, country filters, and source/freshness disclosure.
